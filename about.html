About Me

Hi, I'm Xander.

I'm a student, engineer, and a lover of science — working toward a career in computational neuroscience.

My goal is to bring mathematical rigor and engineering discipline to the fights against age-related cognitive decline, treatment-resistant psychiatric disorders, and brain injury. Medical research is moving toward computational methods, and I want to be ready when it gets there.

I'm double-majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics at Portland State, during which I've had the chance to work on everything from GPU-accelerated retinal imaging pipelines to numerical methods for solving ODEs. I love building things people can actually use — clean interfaces, reproducible environments, efficient algorithms. And I find mathematics genuinely beautiful, especially the corners where it connects to biology and the physical world.
