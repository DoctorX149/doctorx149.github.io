The Multivariate Occupation Kernel Method (MOCK) Library

NSF-RTG in Computation and Data Enabled Science, PSU

This summer, the RTG is investigating efficient methods for data-driven learning of ODE vector fields.

Building on the work of Dr. Victor Rielly's doctoral dissertation on the Multivariate Occupation Kernel Method, our group is seeking to deploy the MOCK algorithm as a publicly available Python and R module for research.

Additionally, we plan to deploy the module to analyze trajectories of Centiloid scores from amyloid PET brain scans, a key biomarker for Alzheimer's Disease.

OCT Retinal Layer Segmentation

IFOCUS Imaging

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) scans are taken in what are known as "B-scans": slices through the depth of the eye. To be useful for medical analysis, volumes of B-scans require transposition to depth-based en-face scans.

At IFOCUS Imaging, I helped modernize a constrained Dijkstra's algorithm graph-search to do just that. Building on the work of Dr. Yukun Guo et al., I achieved efficiency gains of over 90% in segmenting retinal layers from B-scans to create en-face images.

To achieve these results, I translated demo Matlab code into vectorized Python with parallelized C++ bindings for the most computationally intensive step. What's more, I found 60% efficiency gains in the algorithm itself, by replacing the linear graph search frontier with min-heap structure.

OCT-A Retinal Fluid Segmentation

IFOCUS Imaging

Retinal fluid deposits are a leading cause of preventable blindness. Recent advancements in machine learning have allowed for the automated segmentation of these deposits using Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) and Angiography (OCT-A).

At IFOCUS Imaging, I built the pipeline. Using object-oriented design and ONNX, I created a GPU-accelerated inference workflow that exposed a clean interface: hiding pre- and post-processing behind tested components.

With my pipeline, we could seamlessly interchange new ML models as needed. Due to its simple interoperability and GPU-accelerated efficiency, the pipeline enabled rapid testing and prototyping of better models.
